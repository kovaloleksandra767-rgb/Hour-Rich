What is Hour Rich?

The simple explanation

Hour Rich is a set of ready-made instructions for the AI called Claude that turns it from "just a chatbot" into your full-time personal assistant.

Imagine you hired someone who every morning:

  • Checks your email and drafts replies in your voice
  • Prepares a content report — what's working, what flopped
  • Monitors your ads — flags overspend and winners
  • Plans your meals for the week and makes a grocery list
  • Gives you a personalized journaling prompt
  • Sorts files in your Downloads folder
  • Checks in on your clients

And all of this without you doing anything. You wake up — the report is ready. You open your laptop — files are already sorted. You check email — replies are already written. You just hit "send".

💡
Here's all you do: Download a folder, open it in Claude Code, answer 20 questions — and AI starts working for you automatically. Forever.

What are MCP connectors?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. Think of it like a bridge between Claude and your other apps.

Right now, Claude lives in its own world — it can't see your Gmail, your calendar, or your ad account. MCP connectors are what give Claude permission and access to talk to those apps.

An analogy: Imagine Claude is a personal assistant sitting at a desk. MCP connectors are like handing them the keys to your email, your calendar, and your ad dashboard. Without the keys, they can't open those doors. With the keys, they can check your email, read your schedule, and pull your ad data — all automatically.

Are they safe? Yes. MCP connectors use official APIs from Google, Meta, and Notion. You authorize access through the same sign-in screens you already trust (like "Sign in with Google"). Claude only gets the permissions you approve — and you can revoke access anytime from your Google/Meta/Notion account settings.

Do they cost anything? No. All the MCP connectors included in Hour Rich are free. You're connecting to services you already use — there's no extra subscription or fee involved.

Don't worry: MCP connectors are optional. Each one you add unlocks more automation, but the system works fine without them — you just get fewer automated features. Start with zero and add them one by one as you're ready.

Installation

Step by step — simple and easy

1

Install Claude Code

Claude Code is an app by Anthropic that lets AI work with files on your computer.

On Windows:

  1. Open claude.ai/code in your browser
  2. Click "Download for Windows"
  3. Run the downloaded file and follow the prompts
  4. Once installed, log in with your Claude account

On Mac:

  1. Open claude.ai/code
  2. Click "Download for Mac"
  3. Drag to Applications folder
  4. Open and log in
🔧
If installation fails:
• Make sure you have Windows 10+ or macOS 12+
• Try running as Administrator (right-click → "Run as administrator")
• Temporarily disable antivirus during installation
• Restart your computer and try again
2

Get a Claude Subscription

Setup Wizard (/hour-rich)

15 minutes — and the system works forever

With Claude Code open in the hour-rich folder, simply type:

/hour-rich

Claude will launch the setup wizard. It asks you one question at a time. Here's what to expect:

Part 1: Your Life (Layer 1)

Claude will ask:

  • "What does your ideal morning look like?"
  • "Do you want daily journal prompts? What themes matter to you?"
  • "Do you meal plan? Dietary restrictions? How many people?"
  • "What personal admin eats your time?"

Part 2: Your Business (Layer 2)

  • "What's your business? What do you sell?"
  • "What platforms do you post content on?"
  • "Do you run ads?"
  • "What email do you use?"
  • "What takes the most time every week?"
  • "Paste 3-5 examples of how YOU write" (captions, emails, DMs — so AI learns your voice)

Part 3: Your Clients (Layer 3)

  • "Do you have clients or students?"
  • "What do you offer them?"
  • "How do you onboard new clients?"
  • "What messages do you send over and over?"

Part 4: Schedule

  • "What time do you want your morning brief?" (default: 7:00 AM)
  • "What day for the weekly report?" (default: Sunday)
  • "What day for meal planning?" (default: Monday)
💡
Tip: If you don't know what to answer, just say "skip" or "not sure, skip this." Claude will simply disable that module. You can always come back and enable it later.

What Happens After

Claude automatically:

  1. Creates config/my-profile.md — your full profile
  2. Saves templates/email-voice.md — your writing style samples
  3. Generates CLAUDE.md — the brain file, customized to you
  4. Creates automated cron schedules — morning brief, weekly report, meal plan
  5. Shows you a full activation report: what's on, what's off, what commands are available
🎉
Done! Tomorrow morning at your chosen time, your first morning brief will be waiting. You don't press anything — it just appears.

If Something Doesn't Work

A fix for every problem

Claude doesn't see any files / doesn't know what Hour Rich is
You opened Claude Code in the wrong folder. Fix: Close Claude Code, reopen it and select the hour-rich folder specifically (File → Open Folder → hour-rich).
/hour-rich doesn't work / "command not found"
Fix 1: Make sure you type it with the forward slash: /hour-rich (not just "hour-rich")
Fix 2: Check that .claude/skills/hour-rich/SKILL.md exists in your folder
Fix 3: Ask Claude: "Show me what skills you can see" — it'll list them
Morning brief doesn't arrive automatically
Reason 1: You're on the Pro plan ($20) — automated crons require Max ($100)
Reason 2: The cron didn't get created during setup. Run /hour-rich again and go through the Schedule section
Workaround: Just type /morning manually each morning
Email module doesn't work
Gmail needs to be connected via MCP (Model Context Protocol). Fix: Ask Claude: "Help me connect Gmail MCP" — it'll walk you through the process step by step.
Claude doesn't write in my voice / sounds robotic
AI hasn't learned your style yet. Fix: Open templates/email-voice.md and add more examples of how you write (5-10 real captions, emails, DMs). The more examples, the more accurate the voice.
I want to disable a module
Just tell Claude: "Disable the [module name]" or "I don't want journal prompts in my morning brief." It'll adjust the settings.
I want to change the morning brief time
Tell Claude: "Change my morning brief time to 8:00 AM" — it'll update the cron.
Something is completely broken and I don't know what to do
Type in Claude Code: "Something isn't working with [describe the problem]. Help me fix it." — Claude can read its own files and diagnose the issue. It can literally fix itself.

Lifehacks

Pro tips to get even more out of Hour Rich

💫 Lifehack #1: Voice Samples Are Everything

The more writing examples you give, the less editing you'll need. 3 examples = okay. 10 examples = AI writes like you. 20 examples = you can't tell who wrote it.

⏱ Lifehack #2: Keep Claude Code Open

While Claude Code is open, files get auto-sorted every 30 minutes. Just keep it running in the background while you work.

📋 Lifehack #3: Check Your Weekly Reports

Every Sunday, open reports/weekly-report-[date].md. It shows how many hours you saved. This is incredibly motivating.

💬 Lifehack #4: Talk to Claude Like a Human

No need for formal prompts. Just type like you'd text a friend: "check my email," "write a check-in for Anna," "what do I have today?"

🚀 Lifehack #5: Combine Commands

You can ask for multiple things at once: "Check my email and give me a journal prompt for today." Claude handles both.

🛠 Lifehack #6: Customize the Skills

All skills are simple text files (.md). Open any one in .claude/skills/[name]/SKILL.md and tweak it. For example, add new file categories for the organizer.

📅 Lifehack #7: Monday = Planning Power Day

On Monday morning you get: morning brief + meal plan + grocery list. It's the most powerful day in the system. Don't skip it.

FAQ

Answers to every question

Do I need to know how to code?
No. You answer questions and type commands like /morning. Zero coding required.
Does this work on Windows and Mac?
Yes. Claude Code runs on both platforms.
What if I don't have clients?
Skip Layer 3 during setup. The system works great with just Layer 1 (Life) and Layer 2 (Business). When clients come along, just run /hour-rich again.
How much does it cost to run monthly?
Only the Claude subscription: $20/month (Pro) or $100/month (Max for automation). Nothing else. Hour Rich is a one-time purchase.
Is my data safe?
Everything runs through the official Claude by Anthropic. Your data is processed according to their privacy policy. No data is sent to third parties.
Can I cancel my Claude subscription and come back later?
Yes. Your Hour Rich settings are saved in the folder on your computer. When you resubscribe, everything will be right where you left it.
Can I use ChatGPT instead of Claude?
No. Hour Rich is built on Claude Code — a specific technology by Anthropic. ChatGPT doesn't support skills, commands, and crons in this way.
Can AI accidentally delete my files?
The File Organizer MOVES files, never deletes them. And it always shows you a plan before moving anything — you confirm every action. In auto-mode, it only moves obvious files (screenshots, PDFs) and logs every action.
How do I update Hour Rich if a new version comes out?
Download the new version, unzip it next to the old one. Copy your config/my-profile.md and templates/email-voice.md into the new folder. Your settings carry over.
Do I need internet?
Yes. Claude runs through Anthropic's cloud. No internet = nothing works.
